# Refund Policy — SkyBridge Airlines

## Refundable Fares
Full-fare Economy, Premium Economy, and Business tickets are refundable minus a $50 processing fee if canceled more than 24 hours before departure.

## Non-Refundable Fares
Basic Economy tickets are non-refundable. Passengers may receive a travel credit valid for 12 months, minus a $75 change fee, if canceled more than 24 hours before departure.

## 24-Hour Risk-Free Cancellation
Any ticket, regardless of fare class, may be canceled within 24 hours of purchase for a full refund, provided the booking was made at least 7 days before departure.

## Refunds Due to Schedule Change or Cancellation by SkyBridge
If SkyBridge cancels or reschedules a flight by more than 2 hours, passengers are entitled to a full refund or free rebooking on the next available flight, regardless of fare type.

## Processing Time
Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 7–10 business days.
