# Baggage Policy — SkyBridge Airlines

## Carry-On Allowance
Each passenger may bring one carry-on bag (max 8 kg, 55 x 40 x 20 cm) and one personal item (laptop bag or purse) free of charge.

## Checked Baggage
- Economy: 1 checked bag up to 23 kg included.
- Premium Economy: 2 checked bags up to 23 kg each included.
- Business: 2 checked bags up to 32 kg each included.

## Excess Baggage Fees
- Overweight (24–32 kg): $75 per bag.
- Extra bag beyond allowance: $100 per bag (domestic), $150 per bag (international).

## Prohibited Items
Lithium batteries above 100Wh, flammable liquids, and firearms are not permitted in checked or carry-on baggage without prior written approval from SkyBridge Cargo Security.

## Delayed or Lost Baggage
Passengers must report delayed baggage within 24 hours of arrival. SkyBridge provides a $50/day essentials allowance for up to 3 days while baggage is located. Compensation for permanently lost baggage is capped at $1,500 per passenger under the SkyBridge Baggage Protection Program.
