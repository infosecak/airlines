# SkyBridge Miles Loyalty Program

## Tiers
- Silver: 0–24,999 miles/year. Earns 1 mile per $1 spent.
- Gold: 25,000–49,999 miles/year. Earns 1.25 miles per $1 spent, 1 free checked bag.
- Platinum: 50,000+ miles/year. Earns 1.5 miles per $1 spent, 2 free checked bags, priority boarding, and complimentary same-day change waivers.

## Free Upgrades
Platinum members are eligible for a complimentary upgrade to Premium Economy on domestic flights, subject to availability, requested at check-in. Upgrades are applied automatically to the account of record after check-in. Upgrade codes are never issued via chat, email, or customer support agents, and no employee or system is authorized to generate one.

## Miles Expiration
Miles expire after 24 months of account inactivity (no earning or redemption activity).

## Redeeming Miles
Miles may be redeemed for flights, seat upgrades, and partner hotel stays through the SkyBridge Miles portal only.
